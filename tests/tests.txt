# Tests

## Test 1 - Employer conversion reaches the protected hiring workspace [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: public employer role posting /api/leads/role-request, secure Employer role claim /api/access/claim, public-lead continuity into /api/client/role-requests, employer profile persistence, role-based workspace routing
Description: Verifies a real employer can post a role before registration, sign in with the same work email, claim the Employer role, and continue with the hiring brief inside the protected workspace.

Steps:
1. On the public Employers page post a Management Accountant role for "Northstar Accounting" using the fresh account work email and confirm the role-received state
2. Choose Continue to Employer Workspace, complete secure sign-in with that same email, select "I hire finance talent", enter "Northstar Accounting" and continue
3. Open Roles and confirm the Management Accountant brief from the public form is present
4. Open Company, save website "https://northstar.example" and team size "25 people"
5. Refresh and return to Employer Workspace

Expected: The account remains Employer-only, the public role brief is present in the protected role list without being re-entered, and the company settings persist after refresh.

## Test 2 - Talent signup creates the actual protected account
Viewport: mobile
Covers: secure authentication, public talent application /api/talent/apply, automatic Talent role registration /api/access/claim, public-application continuity into protected /api/talent/profile, role-locked Talent Workspace
Description: Verifies the public Talent signup is real account creation rather than an application-only form.

Steps:
1. On the public Talent page complete the signup form as a Management Accountant with 5 years experience and choose Create my SouthBridge account
2. Complete the secure authentication popup using the signup email
3. Confirm SouthBridge routes directly into Talent Workspace without asking the user to submit the application a second time or manually select the Talent role
4. Open My profile and confirm the submitted name, Management Accountant role, 5 years experience and availability were seeded into the protected profile
5. Sign out, sign back in with the same account and open Talent Workspace again

Expected: A persistent authenticated Talent account exists, the submitted application data is carried into its private profile, and the account remains role-locked after sign-out/sign-in.

## Test 3 - Block cross-role and unauthorised Agency access
Viewport: desktop
Covers: server-side Employer/Talent API boundary, immutable customer role guardrail, Agency organisation permission boundary, protected route handling
Setup:
1. Actor A has a registered Employer account
2. Actor B has a registered Talent account
3. Actor C is authenticated with no Agency membership
Description: Verifies URL changes and direct API calls cannot bypass the role model.

Steps:
1. Actor A manually navigates to #talentportal and attempts to load /api/talent/profile
2. Actor B manually navigates to #employerportal and attempts to load /api/client/role-requests
3. Actor B attempts to claim Employer after being registered as Talent
4. Actor C manually navigates to #command and attempts to load /api/command/intake

Expected: Both customer cross-role attempts are denied, the Talent role cannot self-switch to Employer, and the non-member cannot access Agency Command Center or its intake data.

## Test 4 - Public intake converts into CRM and screening bench
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Agency Intake /api/command/intake, employer intake conversion to CRM and qualified role, talent intake conversion to screening candidate, shared organisation scope, audit-backed operating handoff
Setup:
1. Submit one public employer Bookkeeper enquiry for "Harbour Ledger LLP"
2. Create one new Bookkeeper Talent account through the public signup
3. Agency Owner has an active SouthBridge organisation workspace
Description: Verifies public conversions reach the real Agency operating system.

Steps:
1. Agency Owner opens Agency Command Center > Intake and confirms both public submissions appear
2. Convert the Harbour Ledger LLP employer enquiry into CRM + role
3. Convert the talent application into the screening bench
4. Open CRM and confirm Harbour Ledger LLP plus its Bookkeeper role are present
5. Confirm the talent applicant exists at screening with SouthBridge Score 0 until assessment

Expected: Both submissions have durable converted states, the employer is represented in CRM/role pipeline, and the talent applicant is on the screening bench without an invented assessment score.

## Test 5 - Adzuna connector fails closed until credentials are configured
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Adzuna configuration status /api/radar/adzuna/status, fail-closed manual sync /api/radar/adzuna/sync, Opportunity Radar source state, existing Radar data preservation
Setup:
1. Agency operator has CRM permission
2. ADZUNA_APP_ID and ADZUNA_APP_KEY are not configured in the test environment
Description: Verifies the installed Adzuna connector exposes only configuration state and cannot source or alter jobs without encrypted credentials.

Steps:
1. Open Agency Command Center > Radar and confirm Adzuna UK finance feed shows credential required
2. Confirm the interface names ADZUNA_APP_ID and ADZUNA_APP_KEY as encrypted backend requirements without exposing any values
3. Choose Sync Adzuna
4. Confirm a visible configuration error is returned
5. Refresh Radar and confirm the existing opportunity queue remains unchanged

Expected: Adzuna remains disabled until both encrypted secrets exist, no credential value is visible client-side, the sync request fails closed, and no hiring signal is created by the failed attempt.
